#+TITLE: Reusable Data Controls
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

[[file:Relation.lean][Relation]] builds Cedar attribute comparisons without a parallel entity
model. [[file:Lakehouse/README.org][Lakehouse]] owns a location-based data-access boundary
that can be composed with financial or other industry rules.
[[file:Lineage.lean][Lineage]] validates an ordered, acyclic catalog of source and derived
artifacts and computes the exact descendants affected when a source is
withdrawn from a selected use. [[file:LineageUse.lean][LineageUse]] is a POO-owned
Cedar veto for selected actions. It binds the requested artifact and lineage
revision to the resource, then requires the Host-projected availability fact.
[[file:ArtifactAssessment.lean][ArtifactAssessment]] checks whether a Host-supplied
de-identification or model-review claim covers one selected artifact digest,
assessment kind, expected assessor, governance revision, and time. The Host must authenticate
the claim and the actual bytes; constructing a claim in Lean does not do so.
The [[file:../../Examples/Health/MultiHospitalAI/README.org][multi-hospital AI example]] composes that control with independent research
and treatment owners.
The [[file:../Pseudonymization/README.org][top-level Pseudonymization capability]] composes data lineage with
admission and governance for transformation, correlation, release, and
exceptional recovery.

The consuming application defines its dataset catalog, authenticates
projected facts, and enforces any data-plane effect. These modules do not
perform queries, tokenization, cryptographic key management, deletion, or
durable revocation. A change of consent for one use does not erase a source
record or automatically revoke access under another lawful purpose.
