#+TITLE: Reusable Governance Controls
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

[[file:ScopedApproval.lean][ScopedApproval]] binds one approval to its
approver, actor, operation, purpose, source, target, revision, and expiry.
Applications authenticate the approver and supply the revision and clock.
The [[file:../../Examples/Health/Pseudonymization/README.org][hospital pseudonymization example]] derives Cedar approval facts from
these values rather than accepting an unrelated request Boolean.

[[file:Veto.lean][Veto]], [[file:MemberGrant.lean][MemberGrant]], and
[[file:SeparationOfDuties.lean][SeparationOfDuties]] construct owned Cedar
policies for negative controls, membership grants, and actor conflicts.

[[file:Personnel/README.org][Personnel]] owns human organization status and
asset-operation admission, including Agent requests with a human origin.
