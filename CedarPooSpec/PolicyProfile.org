#+TITLE: Policy Profiles as First-Class POO Objects
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

[[file:PolicyProfile.lean][PolicyProfile]] is a Cedar-specific adapter over
LeanPOO's public =Object.define= and =extendWith= APIs. One typed setting slot
is direct. An inherited computed slot reads the final object's setting and
renders one Cedar policy. A child therefore replaces a setting without
copying the policy expression or mutating the parent object.

The caller owns the renderer and the policy ID. Cedar still validates the
resulting policy and decides authorization. This adapter does not infer
which configuration is safe, authenticate an administrator's change, or
install a policy in an external service. A consumer should use the rendered
policy in a real POO policy owner, then inspect its exact revision delta.

[[file:../Examples/Enterprise/AWS/FinancialServices/Reconciliation/README.org][AWS reconciliation]] uses one profile for the Gateway confidence threshold
and another for the correspondence action set. Their independent revisions
compose into a joint incident while the other owners remain inherited.
