#+TITLE: Agent Disclosure Across Derived Data and Channels
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

* Published motivation

[[https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14003][FLOWSEAL]] describes collaborative-workspace lures and channel decoupling in
LLM-agent privacy leakage. Its proposed boundary is a tool interceptor outside
the model's context, informed by source provenance. [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12341][OCELOT]] treats leakage as
cumulative across a trajectory and proposes a verifier and shared budget.
[[https://traefik.io/blog/implementing-forresters-aegis-framework][Traefik's AEGIS implementation article]] motivates an explicit tool-call gate;
this example does not equate gateway masking with source-aware disclosure.
[[https://docs.cloud.google.com/sensitive-data-protection/docs/pseudonymization][Google Sensitive Data Protection]] motivates the preceding hospital tokenization
and join example. A tokenized join does not itself authorize result publication.

These sources motivate the threat and control boundaries. The hospital,
research project, workspace, messages, and policy decisions here are synthetic;
they are not reported deployments or reproduced paper benchmarks.

* Composition and execution boundary

[[file:AgentDisclosure.lean][AgentDisclosure.lean]] uses =LeanPoo.Object.defineStrict= to give the
hospital source a typed object, then =defineStrictFrom= to combine an independent
research-source amendment with it. The inherited artifact method observes the
final source list, and =joinedResultRetainsBothSources= checks its result.
The research method appends a label to an inherited default instead of
rebuilding the whole artifact.

The policy model has separately owned lineage, scoped approval, and shared
budget gates. =Model.mix= assembles them; an incident adds a local veto and
recovery withdraws it. Only governed, incident, and recovered roots can enter
=prepare=. The broad ancestor is retained as a counterexample in Cedar replay,
not as a deployable release root.

#+begin_src mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
    Hospital --> Joined: inherited source label
    Research --> Joined: source amendment
    Joined --> Governed: lineage + approval + budget
    Governed --> Incident: add veto
    Incident --> Recovered: withdraw veto
#+end_src

The Host supplies authenticated source labels, an observed output digest,
approval revisions, policy revision, budget epoch, and audit readiness. The
model rejects an omitted source label, a substituted output digest, a changed
channel after ticket preparation, a stale approval, and the second release
through another channel after the shared budget is consumed. It cannot infer
true source provenance from model-generated text or prove that an external
audit sink and ledger committed atomically. A deploying Host must bind the
actual output bytes and source receipt, perform atomic ticket redemption, and
persist audit before sending either external effect.

[[file:AgentDisclosureExport.lean][AgentDisclosureExport.lean]] sends the same nine synthetic policy decisions to
the official Cedar Rust validator and authorizer. =just example health
agent-disclosure= checks exact allow/deny outcomes, empty policy errors, and
schema fingerprints. This is policy replay and a finite Host-state model, not
an end-to-end hospital integration or a measured privacy guarantee.
