* Scope

AWS is the publisher and platform owner of the reference implementations in
this directory. The insurance lakehouse and banking business domains belong
to those published examples; this directory does not describe AWS's internal
banking or insurance operations. Each case cites the AWS article, sample
source, or product documentation that supports its claims.
