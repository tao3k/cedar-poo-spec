Gateway Cedar decisions are one stage. JWT validation, outbound credentials,
LOB Runtime admission, and data-plane effects retain separate owners and
must be evidenced independently.

* Lean module dependency

The cases share the [[file:../AgentCore/Gateway.lean][AgentCore Gateway projection]]. The cross-domain scenario imports
both case models through their actual Lean modules and reuses banking's
C4/multimethod lineage; imports are acyclic. A direct import from one
business model into the other would make its local Cedar policy depend on
unrelated business rules. The composite workflow is the point where the
different owners meet.
