* Lean module dependency

The cases share the [[file:../../../../CedarPooSpec/Platform/AWS/AgentCore/Gateway.lean][AgentCore Gateway projection]]. The insurance lakehouse case also
combines [[file:../../../../CedarPooSpec/Data/Lakehouse/LocationBoundary.lean][a general data-location boundary]] with
[[file:../../../../CedarPooSpec/Vertical/FinancialServices/ClaimSummaryVeto.lean][a financial-services claims rule]]. The cross-domain scenario imports
both case models through their actual Lean modules and reuses banking's
C4/multimethod lineage; imports are acyclic. A direct import from one
business model into the other would make its local Cedar policy depend on
unrelated business rules. The composite workflow is the point where the
different owners meet. Its two claims calls share one compiled claims root;
its four bank calls share one compiled bank root. Each batch binds its Cedar
decisions to that root, while the banking multimethod supplies the distinct
LOB requirements for each call.
