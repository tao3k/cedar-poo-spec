[[file:Gateway.lean][Gateway.lean]] defines the shared AgentCore entity types, Gateway action
schema entry, entity data, and scoped Cedar policy constructor. It is the
platform adapter used by the [[file:../FinancialServices/README.org][financial-services scenarios]]. Insurance
and banking policy ownership, transfer lineage, and claim settlement remain
in their business-domain modules.
