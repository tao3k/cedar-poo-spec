#+TITLE: RFC 0007: POO Dispatch at the Cedar Construction Boundary
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

* Contract

Lean-POO C4 resolves ordered policy modules. Its multimethod resolves
construction methods from the C4 profiles of *all* dispatch arguments. The
method result is a =PolicyModules.Edit=, which is compiled into an ordinary
Cedar policy set. Cedar's validator and authorizer remain the authorities
for each published policy and request. The distinction matters because Cedar
combines matching =permit= and =forbid= policies itself; choosing a method
must never replace Cedar's decision or silently omit a required check.

The implemented [[file:../../Examples/Enterprise/Agent/DataFlow/README.org][Data Flow case]] has two independently extensible axes:
source profile and destination profile. The classification owner registers
a source-specialized policy edit. The review owner registers a
destination-specialized policy edit. A derived profile pair selects both
contributions in sparse lexicographic C4 order. The existing publication
module hierarchy then merges the owner branches. Its exported Cedar
policies and the Lean/Rust request receipts remain the observable contract.

* Audit of current complex examples

| Case | Current POO work | Additional dispatch fit |
|------+------------------+-------------------------|
| [[file:../../Examples/Enterprise/Agent/DataFlow/README.org][Agent Data Flow]] | Two owner branches and source/destination policy profiles. | Implemented two-argument construction dispatch. |
| [[file:../../Examples/Enterprise/Agent/Payment/README.org][Agent Payment]] | Account, amount, and origin vetoes merge through C4. | A future policy family with independently extensible account and transfer profiles could use dispatch; the present three fixed vetoes do not require it. |
| [[file:../../Examples/Enterprise/Agent/Chain/README.org][Agent Chain]] | Three-to-five request layers and competing policy edits. | Path validation and Cedar authorization are request-time checks; selecting fewer layers by method dispatch would change the security contract. |
| [[file:../../Examples/Enterprise/Vehicle/Mission/README.org][Mission successor]] | Platform, mission, and evidence owners merge through C4. | The fixed proceed/fallback actions need no generic method ecosystem. A separately validated family of vehicle and mission profiles would be a construction-time candidate. |
| [[file:../../Examples/Health/README.org][Wearable triage]] | Privacy, device security, and clinical AI branches merge through C4. | Its current fixed controls benefit from C4; dispatch becomes useful only if independent device and clinical workflow families are added. |
| [[file:../../Examples/Governance/README.org][Governance]] | Owner, platform, and compliance policies and revisions merge through C4. | Existing Cedar data predicates remain in Cedar; no request-time POO dispatch is needed. |

This audit distinguishes an actual open extension axis from a long list of
fixed rules. Adding =DispatchTable=, =InlineDispatch=, or =StaticDispatch=
without several independent generic operations or a measured repeated-call
cost would add machinery without simplifying the current cases.

* Verification

[[file:../../Tests/Enterprise/Agent/DataFlow/Dispatch.lean][Dispatch.lean]]
checks both owner-local selections, inherited two-axis selection, and an
unknown profile. =just check-tests= compiles those claims. The existing
=just check-cedar-artifacts= compares the emitted policy revisions with the
committed Cedar source. =just check-schema-bound-scenarios= replays the
Data Flow requests through the official Cedar Rust parser, validator, and
authorizer. These gates check behavioral preservation; they do not establish
a dispatch performance improvement.
